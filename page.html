MIDDLE EAST

Houthi drone hits Riyadh's tallest tower a second time

RIYADH — A Houthi drone struck the 80-storey PIF Tower in Riyadh's financial district on Tuesday, the second hit on Saudi Arabia's tallest skyscraper in under a week. Saudi Civil Defence said 11 people were injured; the Saudi-led coalition condemned the strike as a terrorist attack.

MYANMAR · THE HAGUE

Fourteen nations back ICJ ruling of Rohingya genocide

The United Kingdom, Spain, Canada, Australia and ten other countries affirmed the International Court of Justice decision that Myanmar's military committed genocide against the Rohingya minority.

UKRAINE WAR

Ukraine strikes Russian vessel in Gulf of Finland

President Zelenskyy confirmed Ukraine struck a Russian vessel in the Gulf of Finland. Kyiv also said a Russian air strike on Zaporizhzhia killed two people and wounded more than 40.

EUROPE · SANCTIONS

EU unveils 22nd sanctions package against Russia

Brussels proposed a 22nd package of sanctions over the invasion of Ukraine, this time targeting Russia's banks, soldiers and athletes.

UNITED NATIONS · AI

Guterres seeks global AI guardrails within a year

UN secretary-general Antonio Guterres urged member states to agree, within the next year, a multilateral framework for managing the risks of artificial intelligence.

MARKETS · NEW YORK

Dow closes above 50,000 as AI sell-off deepens

The Dow rose 0.85% to 50,018.70, its first close above 50,000, even as the Nasdaq fell 1.48%: Nvidia slid 5.86%, the chip index lost 7.88%, and Anthropic dropped below its $83 IPO price.

NEPAL

Magnitude 5.6 quake rattles Nepal near China border

A magnitude 5.6 earthquake struck Koshi province in eastern Nepal, close to the border with China. There were no immediate reports of casualties.
